2025 First Author Featured

First-Principles Study of Polarons in Multiple Crystal Phases of Bismuth Vanadate: Formation, Structure, Transport, and Optical Transitions

Xiao Jiang, Xu Cheng, Zhanqi Liu, Liyun Ding, Weihua Han*.

menu_book The Journal of Physical Chemistry C 129.42 (2025): 19190–19198.
description

Polaron formation, optical transitions, and transport mechanisms in bismuth vanadate (BiVO₄) polymorphs are systematically investigated via DFT+U. We elucidate the microscopic origin of polaron hopping barriers across monoclinic scheelite, tetragonal scheelite, and zircon phases.
